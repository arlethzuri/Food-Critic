--- Answer ---
Among the 139 restaurants with both a Google rating and matched SLCHD inspection history, there is a **weak negative correlation** between rating and total cited violations: **r = −0.246**.

In practical terms, higher-rated restaurants tend to have somewhat fewer recorded violations, but the relationship is weak and there is substantial variation—ratings alone are not a reliable indicator of violation history.

For **critical violations**, the relationship is even weaker: **r = −0.101**. The matched restaurants averaged **4.24 stars**, **38.35 total cited violations**, and **10.75 critical-violation citations** across their available inspection histories.
