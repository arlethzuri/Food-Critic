Question: what does the inspection score mean?

--- Answer ---
An **inspection score is the total violation points assessed during that inspection visit**.

- **0 = clean** — no violation points recorded.
- **Higher scores = more/worse violation points** for that visit.
- It is not a 0–100 “grade,” so a higher number is **not** better.

For context from the data dictionary, inspections with cited violations average **17.6 points**, compared with **1.7 points** for inspections categorized as clean.
