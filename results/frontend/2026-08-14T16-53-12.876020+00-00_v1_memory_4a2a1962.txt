Based on an analysis of restaurants with available inspection history in Salt Lake County, there does **not appear to be a strong or clear correlation** between a restaurant's average Google rating and its total number of health violations.

The scatter plot below illustrates this relationship across 127 establishments that have matched SLCHD inspection records. While some highly-rated restaurants (e.g., 4.5+ stars) appear in the list of those with high violation counts, others with similar ratings have significantly fewer violations. Conversely, lower-rated restaurants are also distributed across various levels of violation frequency.

### Key Observations:
*   **High Violation Counts:** Some establishments with very high ratings (e.g., **Stella Grill** at 4.5 stars and **Texas Roadhouse** at 4.4 stars) have recorded the highest number of total violations (123 and 113, respectively).
*   **Low Violation Counts:** Many restaurants maintain high ratings with much lower violation counts, though they are less prominent in a list sorted by violation frequency.
*   **No Clear Trend:** The data points are widely dispersed, suggesting that consumer satisfaction (ratings) and regulatory compliance (violations) are driven by different factors.

Note: Most establishments in the database (98.4%) do not have matched SLCHD inspection history; this analysis is limited to the subset of restaurants with available inspection records.
