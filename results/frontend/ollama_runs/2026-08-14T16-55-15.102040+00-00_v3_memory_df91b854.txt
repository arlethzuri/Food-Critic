Based on an analysis of restaurants with available inspection data in Salt Lake County, there does **not appear to be a strong or consistent correlation** between customer ratings and the number of health violations recorded.

While one might expect highly-rated restaurants to have fewer violations, the data shows significant overlap: some top-rated restaurants (e.g., 5.0 stars) have high violation counts, while some lower-rated restaurants maintain relatively clean records.

### Key Observations:
*   **High Ratings with High Violations:** Some establishments with very high average ratings (4.5+) have accumulated a large number of total violations over time. For example, **K-Recipe** (5.0 stars) has 36 total violations, and **Siegfried's Delicatessen** (4.7 stars) has 64.
*   **Low Ratings with Low Violations:** Conversely, some restaurants with lower average ratings have relatively few recorded violations. For instance, **Shake Shack** (3.5 stars) shows 29 total violations, which is comparable to many higher-rated establishments.
*   **Clean Records at High Ratings:** There are also examples of "safe bets" where high ratings coincide with very low violation counts, such as **La Yaquesita** (4.8 stars, 1 violation).
