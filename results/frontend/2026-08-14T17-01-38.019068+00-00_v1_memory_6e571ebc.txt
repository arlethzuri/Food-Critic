Question: What does an inspection score mean?

--- Answer ---
Based on the available data, an **inspection score** represents the results of a health inspection conducted by the Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD).

Key details about the score include:
*   **Scale Interpretation:** A lower score is better. A score of **0 indicates a "clean" inspection** with no violations found. As the score increases, it indicates a higher number or severity of violations.
*   **Source:** These scores are part of the official SLCHD inspection records for matched establishments in Salt Lake County.

While the score itself quantifies findings, the specific types of issues found (such as "critical" vs. "non-critical" violations) are recorded separately in the inspection history.
