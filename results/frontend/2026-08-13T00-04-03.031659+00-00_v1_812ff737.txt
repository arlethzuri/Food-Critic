**Cold holding** is the inspection category for keeping refrigerated, potentially hazardous foods cold enough while they are stored or displayed.

In this dataset, it appears as violation code **4.3.59**, labeled **“Cold Holding*”** and marked **critical**. Inspector notes show examples such as cut tomatoes held at **46–52°F**, gyro meat at **47°F**, and milk/rice pudding at **52°F**.

In practical terms, the category means the inspector found food being held too warm in equipment such as a reach-in cooler or make table, which can increase food-safety risk. The chart shows that the cited “Cold Holding” category is concentrated under code **4.3.59**.
