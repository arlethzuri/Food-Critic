--- Answer ---
The difference between a critical and a non-critical violation is that a critical violation is a violation of a PRIORITY ITEM or PRIORITY FOUNDATION ITEM, which can pose an immediate risk to public health, while a non-critical violation is a violation of a CORE ITEM, which is less severe. Critical violations must be corrected immediately or within a specified time frame, while non-critical violations may be corrected within a longer time frame. The inspection score is affected by the number and severity of violations, with critical violations having a greater impact on the score.
