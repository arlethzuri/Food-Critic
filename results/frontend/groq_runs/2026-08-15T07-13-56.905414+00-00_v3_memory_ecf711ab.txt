--- Answer ---
The difference between a critical and a non-critical violation is that critical violations are those that pose a higher risk to food safety, such as improper food temperature or cross-contamination. Non-critical violations, on the other hand, are those that do not pose an immediate risk to food safety, such as minor cleanliness issues. The inspection score is calculated based on the number and severity of violations found during an inspection, with critical violations carrying more weight than non-critical ones.
