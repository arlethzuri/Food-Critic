- **Positive bars** indicate more critical-violation citations in the latest observed year than in the first observed year.
- **Negative bars** indicate fewer citations.
- This is an **endpoint comparison**, not a continuous annual trend: some locations’ latest available inspection year is 2025 rather than 2026.
- A change in citations does **not by itself establish current food-safety conditions**; inspection timing and the number of inspections can differ by establishment.
