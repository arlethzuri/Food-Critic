--- Answer ---
In Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) records, an **inspection score** represents the **total cumulative violation points** assigned to an establishment during a single inspection visit.

---

### Key Rules & Interpretation

1. **Scale Direction (Lower is Better)**:
   - **Score = 0**: A completely clean inspection with zero cited violations.
   - **Higher Scores**: Indicate more violations, higher violation occurrences, or more severe food safety risks.

2. **Violation Severity & Point Weights**:
   - **Non-critical violations** (e.g., general cleanliness, minor facility maintenance) typically carry **~1 point** per occurrence.
   - **Critical violations** (e.g., improper hot/cold holding temperatures, cross-contamination, poor employee hygiene) carry higher weight, averaging **~4.8 points** per occurrence.

---

### SLCHD Inspection Score Summary Statistics
Across **8,020 total inspections** in the database:

* **Minimum Score**: `0`
* **Maximum Score**: `297`
* **Mean (Average) Score**: `10.29` points
* **Median Score**: `7.0` points
* **25th Percentile (Q1)**: `3.0` points
* **75th Percentile (Q3)**: `14.0` points

---

### Score Distribution Breakdown

* **0 (Clean / Perfect)**: **710** inspections (**8.85%**)
* **1 – 5 (Low)**: **2,363** inspections (**29.46%**)
* **6 – 10 (Moderate)**: **1,938** inspections (**24.16%**)
* **11 – 20 (Elevated)**: **1,802** inspections (**22.47%**)
* **21 – 30 (High)**: **604** inspections (**7.53%**)
* **31+ (Very High)**: **603** inspections (**7.52%**)

---

### Average Score by Inspection Type

* **Routine Inspections**: Average score of **10.45** points (*7,543 inspections*)
* **Critical Item Inspections**: Average score of **9.02** points (*216 inspections*)
* **Follow-up Inspections**: Average score of **6.19** points (*254 inspections*) — lower because previous violations were addressed.
* **Complaint Investigations**: Average score of **1.80** points (*7 inspections*)
