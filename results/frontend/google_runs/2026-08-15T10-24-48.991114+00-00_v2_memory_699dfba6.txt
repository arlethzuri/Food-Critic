### Executive Summary & Key Health Department Statistics

In Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) inspection logs, **inspection scores represent demerit points** accumulated during a visit (where **0 represents a perfect, violation-free inspection**, and higher scores indicate a greater volume or severity of cited violations across Priority, Priority Foundation, and Core items under Utah Regulation R392-100 / FDA Food Code 2013).

* **Total Inspections Analyzed:** 7,798 inspection visits across 1,291 SLCHD-registered establishments.
* **Mean Inspection Score:** **10.29 demerits** ($\sigma = 12.07$)
* **Median Inspection Score:** **7.00 demerits**
* **Score Range:** Min = **0** (perfect record) to Max = **297** demerit points.
