--- Answer ---
Under Utah’s **R392-100 Food Service Sanitation Rule** (based on the FDA Model Food Code) and Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) inspection protocols, food safety violations are divided into two primary categories: **Critical** and **Non-Critical**.

---

### 1. Key Conceptual & Regulatory Differences

| Feature | Critical Violations | Non-Critical Violations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regulatory Term** | **Priority Item** ("Critical 1") & **Priority Foundation Item** ("Critical 2") | **Core Item** ("Non-critical") |
| **Primary Focus** | Direct prevention and reduction of foodborne illness or injury hazards. | General sanitation, facility upkeep, equipment maintenance, and SOPs. |
| **Examples** | Unsafe food temperatures, improper handwashing, unmonitored chemical storage, unclean ice machine interiors. | Dusty ceiling air vents, scratched cutting boards, improperly dried dishes, missing food handler cards. |
| **Correction Timeline** | Immediate or within **72 hours** (Priority) to **10 calendar days** (Priority Foundation). | Within **90 days** or as specified by the health authority. |

* **Priority Items ("Critical 1")**: Directly control hazards (e.g., cooking, reheating, cooling, and handwashing) with no intermediate step.
* **Priority Foundation Items ("Critical 2")**: Support Priority items through management controls, training, equipment availability, or labeling.
* **Core Items ("Non-Critical")**: Cover general physical facilities, cleaning procedures, and routine maintenance that do not directly cause foodborne illness if briefly unaddressed.

---

### 2. How Each Affects the Inspection Score

SLCHD uses a **demerit point system** where **0 represents a perfect, clean inspection** and points are **added** for each violation cited (a higher score indicates poorer compliance).

#### Quantitative Findings from Salt Lake County Data:
* **Point Weight per Violation**:
  * **Critical Violations**: Contributes an average of **~5.13 demerit points** per occurrence (regression slope: $5.13$).
  * **Non-Critical Violations**: Contributes an average of **~1.00 to 1.78 demerit points** per occurrence (regression slope: $1.78$).
* **Score Escalation Comparison**:
  * **0 Violations**: Average score = **1.70**
  * **1 Critical Violation** (0 non-critical): Average score = **4.78**
  * **3 Critical Violations** (0 non-critical): Average score = **17.40**
  * **5 Critical Violations** (0 non-critical): Average score = **34.40**
  * **1 Non-Critical Violation** (0 critical): Average score = **1.02**
  * **3 Non-Critical Violations** (0 critical): Average score = **2.99**
  * **5 Non-Critical Violations** (0 critical): Average score = **5.03**
* **Correlation with Overall Score**: Critical violations have a **0.786 correlation** with total inspection score, whereas non-critical violations have a **0.508 correlation**.
* **Overall Averages**: Inspections citing at least one critical violation averaged a score of **14.95**, compared to **2.66** for inspections with zero critical violations.

---

### 3. Top Most Common Violations in SLCHD Records

Across 36,606 violation occurrences recorded in the Salt Lake County database (**10,744 critical** and **25,862 non-critical**):

#### Top Critical Violations Cited:
1. **Unseparated employee drinks in food prep areas**: 482 occurrences
2. **Unclean interior of ice machines**: 443 occurrences
3. **No hand-drying provision at handwashing sink**: 274 occurrences
4. **Unclean soda dispenser nozzle surfaces**: 272 occurrences
5. **Unlabeled chemical spray bottles**: 269 occurrences

#### Top Non-Critical Violations Cited:
1. **Dusty ceiling air vents**: 792 occurrences
2. **Food equipment not air-dried before nesting**: 580 occurrences
3. **Dirty fan covers in walk-in cooler**: 497 occurrences
4. **Missing current food handler cards**: 491 occurrences
5. **Scratched/scored cutting boards**: 425 occurrences
