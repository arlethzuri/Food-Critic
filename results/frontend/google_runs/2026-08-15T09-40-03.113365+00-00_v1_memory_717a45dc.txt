Among restaurants in Salt Lake County with matched Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) inspection records (**139 restaurants**, representing **1.62%** of the 8,592 Google Maps establishments in the dataset; **98.38%** have no matched SLCHD inspection history), there is **only a weak-to-negligible correlation** between consumer Google ratings and health violations.

### **Key Correlation Statistics**

* **Overall Violations per Inspection vs. Google Rating:** **$r = -0.230$** (weak negative correlation)
  * Higher-rated restaurants average slightly fewer total violations per inspection, but the relationship is weak.
* **Total Violations vs. Google Rating:** **$r = -0.246$**
* **Critical Violations per Inspection vs. Google Rating:** **$r = -0.066$** (no meaningful correlation)
  * A high Google rating does **not** indicate fewer critical health/food safety violations.
* **Average Inspection Score vs. Google Rating:** **$r = -0.041$** (no correlation)

### **Summary Insights**
1. **Total Violations:** Restaurants rated **< 3.5** average **6.10 violations per inspection**, whereas top-rated restaurants (**4.5–5.0**) average **3.81 violations per inspection**.
2. **Critical Violations:** Across all rating tiers between **3.5 and 5.0**, critical violation rates remain almost constant at **1.21 to 1.24 per inspection**.
3. **Takeaway:** Customer reviews primarily reflect food quality, service, and ambiance rather than back-of-house sanitation or critical food safety compliance.
