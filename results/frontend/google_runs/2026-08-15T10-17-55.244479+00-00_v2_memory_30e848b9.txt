### What Does "Cold Holding" Mean?

In food safety regulations—including **Utah’s R392-100 Food Service Sanitation Rule** and Section **3-501.16 of the FDA Food Code**—**cold holding** refers to the required temperature control practice of keeping refrigerated **Time/Temperature Control for Safety (TCS)** foods (formerly called *potentially hazardous foods*) at or below **41°F (5°C)** during storage, display, and service.

---

### Key Requirements & Rules

1. **Temperature Standard**: Cold TCS foods must be maintained at **41°F (5°C) or lower** at all times, except during active preparation, cooking, cooling, or when following a formal written procedure under § 3-501.19 (*Time as a Public Health Control*).
2. **Target Foods (TCS Foods)**: Perishable foods capable of supporting rapid bacterial growth, such as:
   - Raw and cooked meats, poultry, seafood, and eggs
   - Cut produce (e.g., sliced tomatoes, cut leafy greens, cut melons)
   - Dairy products, milk, and soft cheeses
   - Cooked plant foods (rice, pasta, beans, cooked vegetables)
   - Garlic-in-oil mixtures and fresh salsas/sauces
3. **Violation Severity**: Cold holding violations (such as Salt Lake County SLCHD Code **4.3.59**) are classified as **Critical (Priority) Violations** because keeping perishable foods in the "Temperature Danger Zone" (41°F to 135°F) presents a direct public health risk for bacterial growth (*Listeria monocytogenes*, *Salmonella*, *E. coli*, *Staphylococcus aureus*).

---

### Salt Lake County Inspection Findings

In the Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) inspection dataset, **cold holding violations** represent one of the single most frequent critical food safety issues cited by inspectors:

* **Total Citations**: **727 cold holding citations** recorded across **488 distinct food establishments**.
* **Commonly Cited Food Items**:
  * **Cut Produce & Greens** (sliced tomatoes, shredded lettuce, salad greens): **180 citations**
  * **Raw & Cooked Meats** (cooked chicken, sliced deli meats, beef): **94 citations**
  * **Dairy & Milk** (milk containers in reach-in coolers): **88 citations**
  * **Cheese**: **59 citations**
  * **Eggs**: **40 citations**
  * **Salsas & Sauces**: **38 citations**
  * **Other TCS Foods / General Prep Units**: **301 citations**
