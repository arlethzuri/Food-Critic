In Salt Lake County Health Department (SLCHD) inspections, violations are categorized based on public health risk, and **inspection scores are cumulative penalty/demerit points** (where **0 is clean**, and higher scores indicate worse compliance).

---

### 1. Key Differences Between Critical and Non-Critical Violations

* **Critical Violations**:
  * **Risk Focus:** Address direct risk factors for foodborne illness, food contamination, temperature abuse, and improper hygiene.
  * **Common Examples in SLCHD Data:**
    * **Food-Contact Cleanliness & Sanitization:** Unclean ice machine interiors / food-contact surfaces (`code 4.4.82`, 2,112 citations) and chemical sanitization concentrations (`code 4.4.76`, 593 citations).
    * **Employee Hygiene & Practices:** Employee drinking/eating in food prep areas (`code 4.2.18`, 757 citations) and improper handwashing sink usage (`code 4.5.26`, 680 citations).
    * **Temperature Control:** Cold holding violations (`code 4.3.59`, 726 citations) and hot holding violations (296 citations).

* **Non-Critical Violations**:
  * **Risk Focus:** Address general sanitation, physical facility repair, storage habits, and equipment maintenance that do not pose an immediate public health hazard.
  * **Common Examples in SLCHD Data:**
    * **Nonfood-Contact Surface Cleanliness:** Dirty walk-in cooler fan covers or outer surfaces (`code 4.4.85`, 2,176 citations).
    * **Physical Facility Repairs:** Damaged walls, ceiling tiles, or floor seals (`code 4.6.40`, 2,109 citations).
    * **General Maintenance:** Equipment adjustment/repair (`code 4.4.63`, 1,661 citations).

---

### 2. How Each Affects the Inspection Score

#### A. Point Weighting per Occurrence
The inspection score increases based on the specific weight assigned to each violation type:
* **Non-Critical Violation:** Assesses **1 demerit point** per occurrence.
* **2-Star Critical Violation:** Assesses **3 demerit points** per occurrence.
* **1-Star / Standard Critical Violation:** Assesses **6 demerit points** per occurrence.

#### B. Impact on Overall Inspection Score
Because critical violations carry 3× to 6× the point penalty of non-critical items, their presence dramatically increases total inspection scores:

* **Clean Inspections (0 Violations Cited):** Average inspection score of **1.7 points** (median 0 points across 1,108 inspections).
* **Non-Critical Violations Only:** Average inspection score of **3.1 points** (median 3.0 points across 2,073 inspections).
* **Has Critical Violations:** Average inspection score jumps to **15.0 points** (median 12.0 points across 4,839 inspections).
