In Utah Food Sanitation Regulation (R392-100 / FDA Food Code) and Salt Lake County Health Department inspections, violations are split into Critical items (Priority and Priority Foundation) and Non-Critical items (Core).

### Key Differences:
1. **Critical Violations (Priority & Priority Foundation Items)**:
   - **Priority Items ('Critical 1')**: Direct risk to public health. These contribute directly to preventing, eliminating, or reducing hazards associated with foodborne illness (e.g., proper cooking/cooling temperatures, hot/cold holding, employee handwashing, and cross-contamination). Corrective action is required immediately or within 72 hours.
   - **Priority Foundation Items ('Critical 2')**: Operational controls, infrastructure, and procedures that support priority items (e.g., personnel training, HACCP plans, equipment/sink availability, documentation, and proper thermometers). Must be corrected within 10 calendar days.

2. **Non-Critical Violations (Core Items)**:
   - Items related to general sanitation, facility maintenance, physical structure, equipment design/cleanability, and general storage. Non-critical items must generally be corrected within 90 days or by the next routine inspection.

### Effect on Inspection Scores:
Salt Lake County inspection scores operate on a penalty system where **0 indicates a clean inspection** and **higher scores indicate worse compliance**:
- **Critical Violations**: Contribute significantly more to the total inspection score, averaging an addition of **~5.13 points** per critical occurrence.
- **Non-Critical Violations**: Contribute fewer points, averaging an addition of **~1.78 points** per non-critical occurrence.
- **Score Impact Breakdown**:
  - **0 Critical Violations**: Average inspection score of **2.66**
  - **1 Critical Violation**: Average inspection score jumps to **8.50**
  - **2 Critical Violations**: Average inspection score reaches **14.72**
  - **3 Critical Violations**: Average inspection score reaches **21.38**
  - **4+ Critical Violations**: Average inspection score spikes to **34.97**
