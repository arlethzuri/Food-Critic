## Critical-violation trends, last 5 years (July 2021 – July 2026 window in this data)

**Scope:** Of restaurants with a matched SLCHD inspection history, **1,120** have at least 2 distinct years of inspections in the last 5 years (a trend needs ≥2 data points). Among those:
- **231 (≈21%)** show a rising trend in critical violations (regression slope > 0)
- **637 (≈57%)** show a falling trend (slope < 0)
- **252 (≈22%)** are essentially flat

So most establishments with enough repeat visits are trending *cleaner*, but a meaningful minority are getting worse.

**Biggest increases** (critical violations per year, slope):
- Ramen Ichizu, SLC — +9.0/yr (2025→2026)
- Sagato Bakery & Cafe, Midvale — +3.3/yr (2022→2026), 47 total critical violations, most recent inspection score of 1 (very poor)

**Biggest decreases** (i.e., most improved):
- Valley Bowling Bar, Taylorsville — −7.0/yr
- Leprechaun Pub and Grill, SLC — −6.0/yr
- Family Breakfast, Draper & El Jaripeo 8, Magna — −5.0/yr each

- Penny Ann's Cafe, SLC — −3.3/yr over all 5 years

A few caveats grounded in the data: this analysis only covers the **1,120 establishments with ≥2 years of inspections in the window** — the vast majority of Google-matched restaurants (98.4% overall per the tool's own note) have no SLCHD inspection record at all, so no trend can be computed for them, and "increase/decrease" here means the slope of a year-over-year regression on summed critical violations per inspection year, not a single before/after snapshot.
