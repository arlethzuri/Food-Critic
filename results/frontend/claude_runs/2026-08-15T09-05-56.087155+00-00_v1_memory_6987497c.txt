## What an inspection score means

An **inspection score** (from `inspections.inspection_score`, sourced from the Salt Lake County Health Department portal) represents the **total violation points accumulated during a single inspection visit**:

- **0 = clean** — no violations cited during that inspection.
- **Higher = worse** — each cited violation (weighted by severity/critical status) adds points to the total.
- This is confirmed empirically in the data: inspections with at least one cited violation average a score of **17.6**, versus just **1.7** for inspections with no violations cited.

Some context from the actual data (7,798 inspections with a recorded score):
- **Minimum:** 0 (clean)
- **Median:** 7
- **Average:** ~10.3
- **Maximum:** 297 (a severe outlier — most inspections score far lower)

The distribution is heavily right-skewed: most inspections cluster at low scores (0–15), with a long tail of much worse ones. Note this score is specific to **each inspection visit**, not a lifetime rating of the restaurant — a single establishment can have many inspections over time with different scores as conditions change.

Also worth noting: only about **1.6% of establishments in this dataset have a matched SLCHD inspection history** at all (most Google-sourced restaurant listings have no linked inspection score), so "no score" does not mean "clean" — it usually means no inspection data was matched.
