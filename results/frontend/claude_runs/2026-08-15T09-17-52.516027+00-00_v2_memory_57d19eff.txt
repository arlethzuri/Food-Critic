## Inspection Score Analytics — Salt Lake County

**Coverage caveat first:** Of the 8,592 restaurants in the database, only 139 (1.6%) are matched to an SLCHD inspection history — consistent with the 98.4% figure noted for the dataset overall. The stats below are computed from the 7,798 inspection records tied to 1,291 distinct SLCHD-listed establishments (note: this establishment-key universe is broader than the 139 Google-matched places, since inspection records exist even where no Google listing was matched).

### Summary statistics (inspection_score: 0=clean, higher=worse, confirmed via glossary — inspections with any cited violation average 17.6 vs 1.7 for clean ones)
- **Mean:** 10.29 | **Median:** 7 | **Std dev:** 12.07
- **Range:** 0 (clean) to 297 (a major outlier)
- Score distribution is heavily right-skewed: 710 inspections (9%) scored a perfect 0, while 2,363 (30%) fell in the 1–5 range and 1,802 (23%) in 11–20. Only 144 inspections (1.8%) scored 41+.

### Charts produced (six facets)
1. **Histogram of all 7,798 inspection scores** — shows the long right tail driven by rare severe inspections.
2. **Score-severity buckets** — most inspections cluster in low-risk 0–10 range (~5,000 of 7,798), but a meaningful chunk (841) land in the 21–40 "needs attention" band.
3. **Average score by year (2009–2026)** — scores rose from ~7 (2010) to a peak of ~13 (2015), stayed elevated (11–12) through 2022–2024, then dropped to ~9 (2025) and ~6 (2026, partial/limited data — treat cautiously).
4. **Average score by inspection type** — Routine inspections (7,380 of them) average 10.4; Followups (211) average only 6.2 (as expected — verifying fixes); Critical Item inspections (202) average 9.0; Complaint Investigations (5, small sample) average just 1.8.
5. **Top 15 establishments by most recent inspection score** — led by two Arctic Circle locations (score 19 each, in Murray and West Jordan), followed by another Arctic Circle (16), Texas Roadhouse and Village Inn (14 each). Notably several high-rated places appear here (SLC Eatery 4.6★ at score 14, Chick-fil-A 4.3★ at score 14), showing inspection score is largely independent of customer rating.
6. **Top 15 by all-time critical violations** — Sagato Bakery & Cafe tops the list with 47 critical violations across its history (despite a 4.5 rating and current score of just 1), followed by Stella Grill and Texas Roadhouse (38 each). This highlights that a good *current* score doesn't erase a poor track record.

### Regulatory context
Per Utah's R392-100 Food Code, inspectors must document violations of **Priority Items** (must generally be corrected within 72 hours) and **Priority Foundation Items** (up to 10 days), while **Core Item** violations get more latitude — this tiered structure is why "critical violation count" and "inspection score" are tracked separately: score reflects point totals from a single visit, while critical violations reflect the highest-risk category of citation accumulated over an establishment's full history.
