## Critical Violation Trend Analysis — Last 5 Years (2021‑07‑17 → 2026‑07‑17*)

*\*The most recent inspection date in the data is 2026‑07‑17, so I used that as the anchor and split the 5‑year window into an "earlier" half (2021‑07‑17–2024‑01‑17) and a "recent" half (2024‑01‑17–2026‑07‑17), comparing average critical violations **per inspection** in each half (not raw totals) — raw totals are misleading here because the recent half actually contains more inspections overall (3,470 vs 2,156) across more establishments (1,312 vs 907), so a rate-based comparison is the fair way to detect real trend direction.*

**Important caveat on grounding:** this analysis draws from the SLCHD inspection tables (`establishment_keys`/`inspections`/`violations`), which are only linked to Google-mapped listings (`establishments`) for **1.6%** of restaurants overall. When I tried to pin the top trending names (e.g., "Top Stop #C-23", "Kokopelli's Koffee House") onto the map via `search_establishments`, most had no `slchd_establishment_key` match to a Google listing, so they can't be shown as pins on the interactive map — the numbers below come directly from the inspection records themselves.

### Findings
Restricting to the 659 establishments with at least 2 inspections in **both** halves (enough data to call a real trend):
- **187 restaurants got worse** (higher avg. critical violations/inspection recently)
- **356 restaurants improved** (lower avg. critical violations/inspection recently)
- **116 were unchanged**

**Most worsened** (biggest rate increase): Top Stop #C‑23 (SLC, 0.5→4.0 crit/inspection), Arivo Cafe (Kearns, 0→2.4), Manolis (SLC, 1.3→3.7), Wendy's #6098 (West Jordan, 0.7→2.75), Garden Espresso (Murray, 1.3→3.3).

**Most improved** (biggest rate decrease): Kokopelli's Koffee House (Holladay, 5.5→1.0), Cafe Rio – West Valley City (5.7→1.2), Jersey Mike's Subs – Herriman (5.75→1.4), Rancheritos Draper (6.2→2.3), Navajo Hogan (South Salt Lake, 4.4→0.67).

The scatter plot shows all 659 restaurants plotted by earlier-period vs. recent-period rate — points below the diagonal improved, points above worsened, confirming decreases (356) outnumber increases (187) overall, i.e. more restaurants trended toward better food-safety compliance than worse over this window.
