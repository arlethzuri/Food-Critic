## Answer

Looking at all restaurants with an average rating of exactly **3.0 stars** (78 establishments total in the dataset) and normalizing their addresses to base street names (stripping house numbers, directional prefixes like N/S/E/W, and suite/unit suffixes), **State St** has the highest count with **11** three-star restaurants, narrowly ahead of:

| Street | # of 3★ restaurants |
|---|---|
| State St | 11 |
| Main St | 10 |
| Redwood Rd | 5 |
| 5600 W | 2 |
| 3900 S | 2 |

State St and Main St stand out clearly above the rest — this makes sense since these are two of the most common commercial thoroughfare names across Salt Lake County/Utah cities (multiple cities each have their own "Main St" and "State St"), so the count aggregates across many different physical streets that share the same name. If you want it broken out by directional segment instead (e.g., "N Main St" vs "S State St" treated separately), the counts are lower and more fragmented (max 6 each), which I can also show if useful.
