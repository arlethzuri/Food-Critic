**Bottom line:** Korean restaurants have a slightly higher average rating (4.52 vs 4.45 for Thai), but I cannot make any violation-count comparison — neither cuisine has any matched inspection/violation records in the SLCHD data, so "more violations" is not answerable from this dataset as currently matched.
