Among the **139 restaurants with both Google ratings and matched SLCHD inspection histories**, ratings show a **weak negative correlation** with violations:

- **Rating vs. total violation occurrences:** **r = −0.249**
- **Rating vs. critical violation occurrences:** **r = −0.105**
- Average rating in this matched group: **4.24 / 5**
- Average total cited violation occurrences: **44.79** per restaurant across its available inspection history.

In practical terms, higher-rated restaurants tend to have somewhat fewer recorded violations, but the relationship is weak—ratings alone are not a reliable indicator of inspection performance. The association is especially minimal for **critical** violations.

The scatter plot shows the substantial overlap: restaurants at similar ratings can have very different violation totals.
