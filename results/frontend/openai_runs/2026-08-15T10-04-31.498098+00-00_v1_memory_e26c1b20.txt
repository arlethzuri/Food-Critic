--- Answer ---
“Cold holding” refers to keeping foods that need temperature control—such as milk or other potentially hazardous foods—properly refrigerated while they are stored or displayed.

In this dataset, it appears as violation code **4.3.59**, labeled **“Cold Holding*,”** and it is marked **critical**. Examples cited by inspectors include milk held at **47°F** in a cold-holding unit and milk left at **58°F** on a counter before being moved into refrigeration.
