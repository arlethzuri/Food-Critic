Question: What does an inspection score mean?

--- Answer ---
An **inspection score** is the **total violation points assessed during one inspection visit**.

- **0 means clean**: no violation points were recorded for that inspection.
- **Higher scores mean more—or more serious—violation points**, so they indicate a worse inspection result.
- The score is specific to that inspection date; it is not a restaurant’s lifetime grade or customer rating.

The chart shows how inspection visits in the dataset are distributed across score ranges.
